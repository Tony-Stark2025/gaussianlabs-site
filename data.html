How We Protect Your IP, Clients & Competitive Edge

You never hand over raw, unredacted customer secrets. Before any archive is licensed for AI training, it passes through a strict three-stage quarantine and legal ring-fence.

1. Automated PII, PHI & Client Redaction

All client names, employee identities, phone numbers, email addresses, bank account numbers, title blocks, and street addresses are programmatically scrubbed and replaced with synthetic tokens prior to settlement.

Your archives are never made public, never resold to industry competitors, and never exposed in searchable databases. Licensing agreements restrict usage exclusively to internal neural network weight training by vetted labs.

No live software agents are installed on your production servers. We work from standard read-only exports or archive snapshots (Git bundles, CAD vaults, ERP backups) verified under mutual NDA before any transfer occurs.
