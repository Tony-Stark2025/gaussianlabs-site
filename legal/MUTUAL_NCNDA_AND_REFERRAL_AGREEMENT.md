### RECITALS
**WHEREAS**, Gaussian Labs operates an institutional data sourcing, technical evaluation, and brokerage network that connects enterprises possessing high-density digital assets (including 3D CAD/BIM assemblies, proprietary codebases, industrial telemetry, and multimodal media) with frontier AI research laboratories and accredited data acquisition platforms (including Mercor, Grepped, Scale AI, and direct foundation model developers);

**WHEREAS**, Partner maintains advisory, fiduciary, IT management, or consulting relationships with enterprise clients ("Prospective Data Licensors");

**WHEREAS**, the Parties desire to collaborate to identify, evaluate, and introduce Prospective Data Licensors to AI data procurement platforms, sharing in referral commissions generated therefrom while strictly protecting confidential business information and preventing circumvention;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### 1. CONFIDENTIALITY & PROPRIETARY INFORMATION
1.1 **Definition of Confidential Information:** "Confidential Information" shall include all proprietary business, financial, technical, or customer data disclosed by either Party or their introduced clients, including client identities, dataset schemas, file counts, IP audit notes, valuation estimates, and the terms of any commercial transaction.  
1.2 **Obligations:** Each Party agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and use it solely for evaluating and executing data licensing introductions. Neither Party shall disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written authorization.  
1.3 **Standard of Care:** Each Party agrees to exercise at least the same degree of care as it uses for its own confidential data, but in no event less than reasonable care.

---

### 2. NON-CIRCUMVENTION COVENANT
2.1 **Non-Circumvention:** For a period of **twenty-four (24) months** from the date of any formal introduction or referral made under this Agreement, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly (through affiliates, agents, subsidiaries, or third parties):  
   (a) Interfere with, circumvent, bypass, or eliminate the other Party from any transaction, partnership, licensing agreement, or discussion with any introduced client, data broker, or AI laboratory;  
   (b) Solicit, negotiate, or conclude any data licensing transaction with any entity first introduced by the other Party without including the introducing Party and paying the agreed-upon fee;  
   (c) Disclose the identity of introduced sources or contacts to any outside entity intended to bypass the commission structure set forth herein.  
2.2 **Procuring Cause Protection:** Any written introduction (including email correspondence, platform ticket submissions, or documented video meetings) citing a specific Prospective Data Licensor shall establish the introducing Party as the exclusive Procuring Cause for that account.

---

### 3. REFERRAL FEE & COMMISSION SHARING STRUCTURE
3.1 **Commission Split:** Upon the successful closing and settlement of any data licensing or acquisition agreement between an introduced client and an AI lab or data platform:  
   * **Gross Referral Commission:** Refers to the total cash payout received from the data platform (e.g., Mercor Data's referral reward up to $100,000, or Grepped's 10% partner commission).  
   * **Partner Commission Share:** Partner shall be entitled to **thirty percent (30%)** of the Gross Referral Commission actually received by Gaussian Labs for that specific closed transaction.  
   * **Lead Sourcing Allocation:** If Partner serves as the primary originating contact who brings the enterprise decision-maker to the table, Partner's 30% cut shall apply across all initial transactions and any renewal/expansion licensing concluded within twenty-four (24) months of the initial close.  
3.2 **Payment Terms:** Gaussian Labs shall disburse Partner's commission share via wire transfer or ACH within **five (5) business days** of receiving cleared funds from the underlying data acquisition platform.  
3.3 **Transparency & Audit:** Gaussian Labs shall provide Partner with written settlement statements and deal confirmation documentation from the data platform verifying the final deal size and commission payout.

---

### 4. TECHNICAL EVALUATION & DATA INTEGRITY
4.1 **Read-Only & Anonymized Ingestion:** The Parties agree that all technical data evaluations performed on Prospective Data Licensor assets shall be strictly read-only and comply with global privacy standards (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and relevant local data protection frameworks).  
4.2 **IP Cleanliness:** No client data shall be submitted to an AI platform without verified, documented certification that the Prospective Data Licensor holds 100% clean, undisputed title to the underlying technical assets.

---

### 5. TERM AND TERMINATION
5.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and remain in effect for two (2) years. Either Party may terminate active partnership discussions upon thirty (30) days' written notice; provided, however, that the **Non-Circumvention and Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination for a full period of twenty-four (24) months** following the date of termination or the date of the latest client introduction, whichever is later.

---

### 6. REMEDIES & GOVERNING LAW
6.1 **Injunctive Relief:** The Parties acknowledge that any breach of Section 1 or Section 2 would cause irreparable injury for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Accordingly, the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief and specific performance, in addition to all other legal remedies.  
6.2 **Liquidated Damages:** In the event of a willful circumvention of this Agreement resulting in an uncredited transaction, the circumventing Party shall pay to the aggrieved Party an amount equal to **one hundred percent (100%) of the total gross fees or economic benefit** realized from the circumvented transaction, plus all reasonable attorney fees and court costs.  
6.3 **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware (or applicable mutual jurisdiction), without regard to conflict of law principles.
